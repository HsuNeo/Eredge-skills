# Tender analysis output schema

Use this as a data-capture and Word-outline reference. Omit empty sections rather than inventing facts.

## 1. Executive bid decision

- Project / tender number / bidder / tender agent / lot.
- Bid deadline, clarification deadline, opening, decryption window, bid-validity end.
- Go/no-go qualification conclusion and unresolved evidence gaps.
- Recommended lot strategy and immediate actions.

## 2. Project and scope

- Location, client, funding, scale, key quantities, duration and defect period.
- Lot/package comparison: covered contracts, value/estimate, work/service scope, interfaces, site offices, staffing.
- Required reports, milestones, deliverables, acceptance and archive requirements.

## 3. Commercial, contract, and standards

- Price type, tax, ceiling/floor, calculation rule, payment method, bid security, performance security.
- Contract risks: staffing replacement, confidentiality, integrity, insurance/safety, liquidated damages, termination, assessment.
- Applicable legal, technical, sector, and local rules.

## 4. Qualifications and scoring

- Enterprise credentials, licenses, joint-bid rules, credit exclusions.
- Similar-project and key-person thresholds, proof-document field requirements, registration and social-security dates.
- Score matrix, price formula, tie-break, award limitations, and evidence plan for every scoring point.

## 5. Submission and materials checklists

Separate the following, each with “Required evidence”, “Owner”, “Status”, and “Notes” columns when appropriate:

1. Business/technical envelope.
2. Price envelope.
3. Platform/CA/guarantee actions.
4. Post-award and contract-start items.

## 6. Disqualification and electronic-bidding controls

Use columns: `Risk / tender basis / consequence / must respond or control / owner / completion`.

Explicit tender grounds commonly include non-compliant form/signature/guarantee, missing qualifications, lack of substantive response, pricing defects, duplicate bids, late or unencrypted upload, failed decryption, failure to answer official clarification, collusion, false documents, or bribery. Record only grounds actually found in the tender.

For electronic controls, separately identify whether the tender expressly regulates IP/device/CA/tooling. If not, phrase IP/device guidance as preventive anti-collusion control rather than an automatic tender rejection rule.
